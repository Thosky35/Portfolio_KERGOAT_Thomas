Polytechnique Montreal

Polytechnique Montreal is one of Canada’s leading engineering institutions, recognized for its strong academic programs, cutting‑edge research, and close collaboration with industry. Located in the heart of Montreal, the university offers a dynamic and multicultural environment, modern facilities, and a wide range of engineering specializations that prepare students for their careers.

  • Location: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
  • Courses: Engineering, Computer Engineering, Aerospace, AI, Applied Sciences
  • Languages: French, English (some programs)
  • Environment: Large urban campus with strong industry partnerships
  • Specialty: AI, aerospace, sustainable engineering, advanced technologies
