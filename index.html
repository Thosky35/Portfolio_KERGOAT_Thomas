Engineering Courses

Four scientific and technical projects completed during my free time, each with a short reflection on what I learned.

TFJM²

In 2024, I took part in the TFJM² (a French mathematics research tournament) with four friends. We worked on open problems, won the regional tournament and finished 5th at the national final.

What I learned: how to reason rigorously on a problem with no known answer, defend my solution orally against other teams, and share the work inside a team.

TIPE: the Van der Pol oscillator

Many natural and engineered systems exhibit self-sustained oscillations, from the human heartbeat to electronic circuits. For my 2026 TIPE, I investigated the Van der Pol oscillator, a mathematical model introduced to describe such phenomena. Through mathematical analysis, numerical simulations and physical interpretation, I explored how a simple equation can capture complex non-linear behaviour and explain the emergence of stable oscillations.

What I learned: mathematics, simulation and physics complement each other, and even complex concepts can be made accessible through a clear and structured presentation.

Games: Minesweeper & Snake

I programmed a Minesweeper and a Snake game. The Snake game is written in C, with a working menu (Play / Options / Quit) and ideas for further improvements.

What I learned: how to structure a program, handle user input, and debug step by step.

Web development

I build websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript: this portfolio, a website about my football club, a site of maths exercises written in LaTeX and 3 other projects.

What I learned: how to adapt an existing template, organise content clearly and fix errors one by one.

Civic Engagement

🎓 Civic Service (SNU)

I took part in the French Service National Universel (SNU), an experience that strengthened my sense of civic responsibility, teamwork and commitment to the community.

Through collective projects, workshops and community service activities, I worked with young people from different backgrounds and learned how to cooperate effectively in new environments. These experiences helped me develop adaptability, leadership and a strong sense of initiative.

The programme also raised my awareness of social and environmental issues, encouraging me to take a more active role in local community projects and volunteering activities.

🏀 Youth Basketball Commitment

I have devoted more than 90 hours to youth basketball, coaching players aged 7 to 15 during training sessions, holiday camps and competitions. Beyond teaching technical and tactical skills, I helped young athletes develop confidence, discipline and team spirit.

I also refereed official matches, strengthening my decision-making abilities, sense of fairness and capacity to take responsibility under pressure.

In addition, I contributed to improving the communication and visibility of my club by redesigning its website and helping manage its social media presence, making information more accessible to players, families and members.

🌍 Community & Environmental Volunteering

I participated in several community clean-up initiatives in Saint-Grégoire, organised by both the municipality and my schools. These activities promoted environmental awareness and collective responsibility while contributing directly to cleaner public spaces.

In late 2023, I also volunteered with Le Panier de Grégoire, taking part in outreach and food-distribution activities for people facing hardship. This experience reinforced my understanding of solidarity and the importance of local volunteer networks.

🤝 What I plan to do

During my studies at ENSEEIHT, I would like to continue my civic engagement through local associations and volunteer work. Having already enjoyed coaching young basketball players, I would especially like to help younger students develop confidence and succeed in mathematics and science.

For example, I could volunteer to provide tutoring for pupils facing academic difficulties, or take part in mentoring programmes designed to support students from disadvantaged backgrounds.

I would also be interested in contributing to local charities or community organisations, particularly those focused on education, social inclusion or environmental protection.

Career project

In the longer term, I am open to several career paths that reflect my interests in technology, mathematics and finance.

One career path that particularly interests me is telecommunications engineering. I would like to contribute to the design and optimisation of communication networks and digital systems. The rapid development of technologies such as 5G, connected devices and future communication infrastructures makes this field both challenging and exciting.

Another career path that appeals to me is quantitative finance. I would like to use advanced mathematical models, statistics and algorithms to analyse financial markets and support investment decisions. This field attracts me because it combines mathematics, computer science and real-world applications.




A third possibility would be to work in financial management. As I enjoy working with numbers and analysing complex situations, I could see myself contributing to the financial strategy of an organisation and eventually taking on responsibilities in positions such as Finance Director.
