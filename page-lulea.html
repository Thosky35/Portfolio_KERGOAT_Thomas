Luleå University of Technology

Luleå tekniska universitet

Luleå University of Technology (LTU) is a leading Scandinavian institution known for its strong engineering programs, innovative research environment, and close collaboration with industry. Located in northern Sweden, LTU offers students a modern campus, high‑quality facilities, and a unique Arctic setting that encourages both academic excellence and personal development.

  • Location: Luleå, Sweden
  • Courses: Engineering, Computer Science, Space Engineering, Industrial Management
  • Languages: English, Swedish
  • Environment: Modern campus with strong industry partnerships
  • Specialty: Arctic technology, sustainable engineering, space research

Why this destination?

Luleå combines a modern engineering university, space research and close links with industry. The quality of life in northern Sweden matches my wish for a balanced life while studying hard.
